# Tartanleaf books, guides and tools

Tartanleaf.com Inc. publishes practical, technically grounded books and maintained resources by [Greg Weir](https://www.tartanleaf.com/about/). The work is written for people who want to understand technology, make informed choices and troubleshoot methodically without unnecessary jargon.

- [Tartanleaf website](https://www.tartanleaf.com/)
- [Books by Greg Weir](https://www.tartanleaf.com/books/)
- [The Tartanleaf Practical-Technology Method](https://www.tartanleaf.com/practical-technology-method/)
- [Free and open-source tools](https://www.tartanleaf.com/tools/)

## Books and series

### Smart Tech for Real People

Practical technology guides for everyday readers. [Home Networking](https://www.tartanleaf.com/smart-tech/home-networking/) and [Digital Literacy](https://www.tartanleaf.com/smart-tech/digital-literacy/) are available now. [Tech for Seniors](https://www.tartanleaf.com/smart-tech-for-seniors/) is scheduled for September 29, 2026.

[Explore the Smart Tech for Real People series](https://www.tartanleaf.com/smart-tech/)

### RadioBook

A five-volume amateur-radio learning path, Canadian and U.S. answer guides, and free browser-based practice games for people moving from curiosity to confident operation.

- [RadioBook books and learning path](https://www.tartanleaf.com/radiobook/)
- [RadioBook Game Suite](https://radiobook.ca/)

### Bitcoin Mining with Bitaxe

A practical guide to open-source home ASIC mining, supported by maintained model, firmware and community updates.

- [Bitcoin Mining with Bitaxe](https://www.tartanleaf.com/bitcoin-mining-with-bitaxe/)
- [Maintained Bitaxe updates](https://www.tartanleaf.com/bitcoin-mining-with-bitaxe-updates/)

## Open-source tools

Tartanleaf tools emphasize understandable results, local control and explicit limitations:

- [HomeNetCheck](https://github.com/gregweir/HomeNetCheck) — one-button home-network checks with plain-language results
- [homenet](https://github.com/gregweir/Home-Network-Troubleshooter) — command-line home-network diagnostics and explanations
- [Tech Passport](https://github.com/gregweir/tech-passport) — local-first personal technology and recovery planning
- [AI Engine Room](https://github.com/gregweir/ai-engine-room) — bounded observations and explanations for supported local-AI runtimes
- [Open Lobby Board](https://github.com/gregweir/open-lobby-board) — self-hosted local-first information display for hotels and inns

See the [Tartanleaf Tools page](https://www.tartanleaf.com/tools/) for screenshots, intended use and important limitations.
